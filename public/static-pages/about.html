An Integrated Island Energy Partner,Not Just a Supplier

Nawah Energies SDN. BHD. is a Malaysian-incorporated energy company headquartered in Penang. The name نواة (Nawah) means nucleus in Arabic, and that is exactly what our Island Grid™ CHP plant is: the central energy nucleus of your island, delivering electricity, fresh water, process heat, and cooking gas simultaneously from a single renewable system.

We are not a product vendor. We are a full-lifecycle energy partner ,from the first feasibility study to 20 years of operations. Every Island Grid™ we build is tailored to the specific needs of the client ,their energy demand, water requirements, cooking gas consumption, and growth plans. Our clients replace diesel permanently, cut costs by up to 30%, and gain recognised green credentials in the process.

Our supply chain runs from Malaysia to any island or remote site in the world. Our technology is proven. Our model is scalable. Our fuel is renewable.
